SIL Language Technology Impact & Use Survey

SIL Language Technology builds tools and fonts that help communities engage with Scripture and flourish in the languages they value most. To prioritize our work wisely, we asked the people who use them — translators, linguists, typesetters, and literacy workers around the world — which tools and fonts matter most, and how they've made a difference. Here's what they told us.

Insights

what the data adds up to

Paratext punches above its weight. 93 encouragement comments against a 64% usage share — the highest encouragement-to-usage ratio among the top products.
Scripture audio is the quiet gap. The least-covered use case (33%) has no dedicated product in the top 10 for encouragement, and its tools (Audio Project Manager, HearThis, Glyssen) form the smallest cluster in the Reach chart.
App builders add up. 515 of 765 respondents (67%) rely on at least one of the four app builders — more combined reach than any single one shows on its own.
Fonts are the quiet majority. 84% of respondents rely on SIL fonts — more than any single named tool, even though fonts rarely get top billing.
The Gratitude chart isn't the whole story. These top 10 products account for 375 of the 385 total encouragement comments (97%) — the remaining 3% are spread across other tools not shown here.
